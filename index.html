What this number means

The percentile is the share of households with financial assets at or below your amount. Households with the same balance share the same percentile.

These are gross financial assets: debts are not subtracted. This is a household comparison, not an individual comparison.

Method & limitations

All five SCF implicates are combined with the official survey weights. The curve is a weighted empirical cumulative distribution: it steps at observed balances, without inventing values between observations. The horizontal axis uses log(1 + assets / $1,000) to show small and large balances together.

Results are survey estimates, not exact population ranks. No confidence intervals are calculated. The far upper tail has limited sample support. Dollar values refer to the survey year, not current purchasing power.
